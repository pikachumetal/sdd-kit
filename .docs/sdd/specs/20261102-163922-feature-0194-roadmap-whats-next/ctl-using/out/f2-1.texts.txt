**What I understood, correct me:**

- You want a waitlist: when someone tries to book a room that is full for a slot, they join a queue for that room and slot.

- I foresee about 3 tasks (join the queue, view and leave the queue, promotion when a slot frees up). That's one surface, so I wouldn't split it.

❓ **What happens when a booked slot is cancelled and someone is waiting?**
Today `cancelar` only prints `cancelada <día> <franja>`. The first person in the queue could get the room automatically, or just be told it's free.
1️⃣ **Automatic promotion.** `cancelar lun 10:00-12:00` prints `cancelada lun 10:00-12:00; Norte pasa a <quien esperaba> (1.º en espera)`. The room is booked straight away. The cost: someone gets a booking they might no longer want, and they have to cancel it.
2️⃣ **Notify only.** The same cancel prints `cancelada lun 10:00-12:00; Norte libre, 1.º en espera: <quien esperaba>`. The person still has to run `reservar`, and someone else could take the slot first.
3️⃣ **Hold for a limited time.** The room stays held for the first in line for N minutes. This is the most complex option, and it needs clocks and expiry, which this CLI doesn't have.

➡️ **I recommend 1️⃣**, because this is a command-line tool with no push notifications. A message printed at cancel time is easy to miss, and the slot would go unclaimed. In exchange, someone can get a booking they didn't confirm.
