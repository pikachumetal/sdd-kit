#### Critical (Must Fix)

**1. Voiding a booking still sends a notice to the responsable: only the "por correo" label was removed**
- **File:line:** `src/app.js:18`, with `src/app.js:28-30` and `src/app.js:36`.
- **What's wrong:** `voidBooking` still runs `outbox.push({ to: 'responsable', body: \`anulada ${room} ${day}\` })`. The notice is still built, still addressed to the responsable, and still queued in the outbox. `salida` (`pending()`) still prints it as `responsable: anulada Norte lun`. The diff only changed how the notice is formatted: no subject and no «(enviado por correo)». The behavior is still there.
- **Why it matters:** The spec's REMOVED requirement «Avisar por correo» asks for this behavior to be gone. The dev-lead decision says the same: «El aviso por correo al anular desaparece: el responsable avisa en persona — "el correo sobra"». Someone running `anular Norte lun` and then `salida` still sees the notice to the responsable. The Intent says nobody reads that notice and it is being removed. The code does the opposite.
- **How to fix:** Delete the `outbox.push(...)` line from `voidBooking`. Once it is gone, nothing writes to `outbox`, so `outbox`, its `export` and `pending()`/`salida` become dead code. Removing them or not is the quality axis's call. At minimum, `salida` must not list anything after an `anular`.
- **Gap:** `contradicts`.
- **Comes from:** REMOVED «Avisar por correo» in the `bookings` delta, and the dev-lead decision «El aviso por correo al anular desaparece». That decision is not covered at all, not even partly.
