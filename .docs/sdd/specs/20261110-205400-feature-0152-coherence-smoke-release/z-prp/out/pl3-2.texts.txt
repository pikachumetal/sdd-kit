#### Minor (Nice to Have)
1. `src/app.js:58`, reading the `--motivo` value. `params[params.indexOf('--motivo') + 1]` has a latent bug when the flag is absent. `indexOf` returns -1, so the expression reads `params[0]`, which is the room. Book rows, as judgement: possible "Clean Code: avoid hidden temporal coupling / functions that depend on call order", and possible "A Philosophy of Software Design: information leakage" (parsing is mixed into the dispatcher).

5. `test/cancel.test.js`, test order. It is fragile to anyone who reorders or inserts tests. A short sequential-state note in the test names would help, or making the last test do its own `cancelar` and then assert, which costs one line. The two tests that precede it (`motivo fuera de la lista` and `sin motivo no cancela`) must stay before the successful cancel, since `libres` expects `Sur` only.
