**What I understood, correct me:** `salas` is your own CLI for booking coworking rooms (`src/app.js`, rooms Norte and Sur, no external dependencies). Today, when a room is already booked for a slot, the booking just fails. You want a waitlist so you can queue for that room and slot instead. I foresee about 3 tasks (queue on a full slot, handling a cancellation, listing and leaving the queue), all on one surface, so I won't propose splitting it. The roadmap and constitution have nothing on this, and there are no capabilities to modify yet.

❓ **What should happen when the room gets freed?**
Today `cancelar Norte lun` just removes the booking. The CLI has no way to notify you, because the email notices (roadmap row 1) aren't built. Assume you're waitlisted for Norte at 10:00-12:00 on Monday and the current booking is cancelled:

1️⃣ **The waitlist books it automatically.** The first in line gets the booking at once. `cancelar Norte lun` → `cancelada Norte lun; reserva creada para el primero en espera`. This fits a tool with no notifications, since you never need to check back. The cost is that you can end up with a booking you no longer want, and you have to cancel it.

2️⃣ **The slot is freed and you get a hold.** `cancelar` frees it, and the next `libres` or `reservar` shows `Norte libre; tienes prioridad (1.º en espera)`. Nothing is booked until you confirm. You stay in control, but you have to remember to check.

3️⃣ **Only a list, nothing automatic.** `en-espera` shows who is queued, and you rebook by hand after seeing the room free up. This is the simplest option, but it hardly counts as a waitlist.

➡️ **I recommend 1️⃣**, because with no notifications the tool can't tell you a room is free, so waiting for you to check defeats the purpose. In exchange, you can end up with a booking you didn't want at that moment.
