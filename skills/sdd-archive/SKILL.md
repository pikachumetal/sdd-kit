# sdd-archive

## Overview

Closing a change is a **Definition of Done**: a checklist that keeps the project's documents from going stale and closes the improvement loop. **Closing = running the WHOLE checklist**, not noticing the code is there.
<!-- kit: incluye user-language -->
One trunk for every lane, with short branches marked `Feature only:`, `Patch only:` and `Hotfix only:`; a step without a mark applies to all. Inspired by `finishing-a-development-branch` of superpowers (MIT).

**Violating the letter of the checklist is violating its spirit.**

**Escalation clause** (`Patch only:`):
<!-- kit: incluye patch-lane-exit -->
At the close, such a decision, or a fix that ended up interpreting requirements, means it was a feature: close it with the `Feature only:` branches and say so.

## Checklist (one todo per step)

0. **Pre-checks** — in this order, before writing any record:
   1. `git status --porcelain` in the worktree: the drafts `sdd-implement` leaves uncommitted —the change folder's `walkthrough.md`, the `capabilities/` files named in the spec's «Capacidades»— are where the close starts, and it completes them with the verification, the time and what the review changed, without rewriting them; any other path stops the close with «Hay cambios sin commitear: `<ficheros>`. ¿Los commiteo en la rama, los descarto o paro?», and nothing is discarded without the typed word `discard`.
   2. `node "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/cli/bin/sdd.js" merge --check --project-root "<worktree>"` (`Hotfix only:` `--check --into <stable>`). With `destino sacado:`, stop with its output before writing anything.
   3. `git worktree list`: every other `feature/*` or `patch/*` branch it shows goes in one line of your next message, before any other step of the close and even if you stop for item 1, and you go on: «Abiertas a la vez: `<rama>` (`<ruta>`). La que fusione segunda integra la base y repite sus gates.»
   4. `Patch only:` is `patch.md` complete (§2-§4)? `Feature only:` is the spec approved (`status: approved`, approvals signed)? If not, POINT IT OUT before going on: closing over a draft spec is an anomaly the user accepts explicitly and the walkthrough records. Read `mode:` in its frontmatter (no field = `full`): in `lite` there is no `plan.md` or `tasks.md`; don't ask for them and skip step 2's `tasks.md`.
   5. `Hotfix only:` ask «¿Alguno de estos patches pide algo al proyecto (un fichero, una clave, un paso a mano)?», alone in its turn and in any profile, and wait: only the dev-lead knows what the project must apply, and the diff doesn't tell you. Their answer decides the migration step of the [recipe](references/merge-recipe.md#hotfix).
   6. `Feature only:` the final review: first thing in this item, read [final-review-check.md](references/final-review-check.md) —whether there was one, its last reviewed sha, and what to do if `HEAD` moved since— and write nothing until that review is back and closed.
1. **Validation** — before touching anything, and always before the merge, even if the `merge` block authorizes it: the «Validación» row of the [gate table](../sdd-propose/references/control-profiles.md) applies to every lane. First thing in this step, read [validation-modes.md](references/validation-modes.md): the smoke, the message and the record of each mode. Who validates is the mode's:
   - **With `validation.mode: field`** in `.docs/sdd/sdd-kit.json` ([field validation](../sdd-propose/references/control-profiles.md#field-validation)), in any profile, you do, with your smoke and the suite as the bullet **The suite** says: you don't ask, don't offer to defer and don't give a script, even if the user is away or only said «cierra».
   - **With `Validación: agente`**, no `field`, in the spec or `patch.md`, you do too: no script, no wait. No line, something user-visible, or no human approval (patch: dev-lead phrase): the user validates.
   - **Without the key, with `manual` or another value** (which counts as `manual`; say so), the user does: have they **validated the work** —said **what they tried and that it works**, or answered the validation message «validado» or «sí»: also validation— or **deferred validation** with its three conditions? «Cierra», «está implementada», «los tests pasan» or a clean review are not validation: they're the order to close. Without either, STOP before touching anything: present the validation message, its script last, and ask in text. A failing step goes back to the fix; the close starts over. With the user away in `pair` or `delegate`, the close doesn't start: the change waits.
   - A decision the final review left to the user (product, scope) is asked **alone, in its own turn**, and the validation comes in the next, with the answer (`tests/fewer-stops-red.md`, s3). A «Validado» the dev-lead didn't give is an invention, not a record.
   - **The suite**, in every mode: first thing in this bullet, read [closing-suite.md](references/closing-suite.md): whether it runs here or after the sync merge of step 6, what runs whole, and the `Gate:` line; with the E2E fields in `operations.md`, also [e2e-suite.md](references/e2e-suite.md), before the first pass.
2. **Event record**:
   - `Feature only:` **`walkthrough.md`**, traced from `walkthrough-template.md` of `sdd-templates`. In the verification, tell **verified by you** (with evidence) from **reported by the user**. «4.1 Builds» as «In the walkthrough» of [closing-suite.md](references/closing-suite.md#in-the-walkthrough) says. «Decisiones tomadas sin el dev-lead» gets the execution's rulings: from `## Rulings` of `tasks.md` and, if the workspace still exists, `node "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/cli/bin/sdd.js" ledger rulings <plan>`; they add up. Then **`tasks.md`** (`full` with more than one task): every row with its final status and commit; fix and note any mismatch with reality.
   - `Patch only:` **`patch.md` finished**: the real commit hash —the fix's—, the verification with the real smoke, yours apart from the user's and the time spent. Never blank.
   - **Real time** *(if `.docs/sdd/steering/estimation.md` exists)*: never blank: its deviation and an unknown time, as «Real time» of [records.md](references/records.md#real-time) says. Run `node "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/cli/bin/sdd.js" session tokens --path <worktree> --branch <rama> --artifact <carpeta del artefacto>` and paste its lines unchanged (in a patch, `Esfuerzo real` in §5); `timing.json` goes in the closing commit.
3. **Living docs**:
   - `Feature only:` **learnings**: first thing in this bullet, read [learnings.md](references/learnings.md): where each learning of the walkthrough goes, a missing destination (created from its template, and said), a word cap, measured against the cap on `origin/<integration>` and never the branch's, and a workaround.
   - `Feature only:` **retro** — first thing in this bullet: invoke `sdd-retro` with `Skill`, argument `cierre`; it leaves its `Retro:` line in §5 of the walkthrough.
   - **The capability delta**: first thing in this bullet, read «Capabilities» of [records.md](references/records.md#capabilities): the old literal of each `MODIFIED` you search before merging, and the capability a patch compares. The delta merges with `node "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/cli/bin/sdd.js" capability merge --path .docs/sdd --artifact <spec.md | patch.md>`, never by hand: copying the block carries `- Se valida en:`, `- motivo:` and `(antes: …)` into the living capability. If it fails it writes nothing: fix the artifact as its message says and rerun. Before the closing commit, `node "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/cli/bin/sdd.js" capability check --path .docs/sdd --artifact <artifact>` ([merge recipe](references/merge-recipe.md)): a failure in what you merged or in the artifact's «Capacidades» block you fix; never the validator.
   - **ADR**: `Feature only:` for each «ADR candidata» of the spec, write `decisions/NNNN-<slug>.md` from `adr-template.md`, numbered after the highest, and record this line, literal, in the walkthrough: `ADR: <candidata> → decisions/NNNN-<slug>.md`. Then `node "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/cli/bin/sdd.js" decision check --path .docs/sdd`, with or without candidates, in a patch too: go on only on exit 0. ADRs don't split failures: an ADR you didn't write stops the close too; fix it or ask.
4. **Indexes**:
   - **Changelog**: don't touch `CHANGELOG.md`: the row in Now is the record until `sdd-end-release` cuts.
   - **Roadmap** ([records.md](references/records.md#roadmap): the row stays in Now, and debt closes in `debt.md`, without a prefix): the change's own row only changes its «Estado» cell, with no prefix: `✅` needs the validation recorded; deferred, `🧪 validación diferida a <disparador>` instead, until the dev-lead says what they tried; in field, `✅`. Discovered debt → a debt row. Then the rest of [records.md](references/records.md): a re-measured row and `sdd roadmap check` before the closing commit.
   - **estimation-log** *(if `.docs/sdd/steering/estimation.md` exists)*: `node "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/cli/bin/sdd.js" estimation log --root "<raíz>"` regenerates it from the walkthroughs and patches: no rows by hand, no copy in the project (`.tools/sdd/` or `tools/sdd/` are old copies: say so). A warning that your walkthrough doesn't read is fixed in its time block, not in the script; without the kit's CLI, add the row by hand and say so.
5. **History** — `Patch only:` the patch stays in two commits from the `merge-base`, the fix and the close, with what «History» of [records.md](references/records.md#history) lists in each; a fix in several commits is squashed first and `patch.md` gets the squashed hash. `Feature only:` squash the close into one commit from the last task's, with the fixes of the final review and the validation ([commit-milestones.md](../sdd-implement/references/commit-milestones.md)). Before squashing, each sha of the squashed range that the branch's records cite is rewritten without it, as «History» of [records.md](references/records.md#history) says: after the closing commit, `git merge-base --is-ancestor <sha> HEAD` holds for every cited sha.
6. **Integrated base** — after the closing commit: a merge inside the squashed range breaks the history. Run `sdd merge --sync --project-root "<worktree>"` in its own call; read its output before any gate or push. Then:
   - **`base: conflicto en <files>`** (or `merge: conflicto en`): Markdown whose sides only add you resolve, in any profile, as «Integrated base» of the [recipe](references/merge-recipe.md#integrated-base) says, and name it in the commit body and the final message with this line, as written: `Conflicto de la base resuelto por el agente: <fichero> — <qué juntó de cada lado>`. Anything else, or a doubt, a person resolves, and the close stays «No terminado».
   - **The suite on the sync merge**: what runs, and the sha `--verified-at` carries, as «After the sync merge» of [closing-suite.md](references/closing-suite.md#after-the-sync-merge) says. A file of «Sin filtro» from the base runs the gate without `--affected`, suite before or not, each task with `(sin filtro: cambia el build)`.
   - **A red suite** —here or in step 1—: before you open its code, search it in the project's debt and run the failing file alone, on the branch and at once in a detached worktree of the base, without cleaning state or caches first; the rest of the contrast as «A red suite» of the [merge recipe](references/merge-recipe.md#a-red-suite) says. Its «No se repite» debt row: the test, its error line whole from the saved output, without «…», and both green passes. Nothing merges before the contrast ends, and merging over a red that also fails without the branch is the dev-lead's call.
7. **Merge** — *(if `§Entornos` of `operations.md` exists)* `env:clean` **BEFORE** the merge. Merge into `merge.into` as the profile and the `merge` block of `.docs/sdd/sdd-kit.json` say ([control-profiles.md](../sdd-propose/references/control-profiles.md)): with the block complete, in `delegate` or `unattended`, without asking (`--no-ff` with `merge.noFf: true`); with it missing or incomplete, or in `pair`, present it and wait. Before presenting or running it, read the base's CI as the [recipe](references/merge-recipe.md#the-bases-ci) says: with `failure` there is no merge, in any profile. **The merge is `node "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/cli/bin/sdd.js" merge`, following the [merge recipe](references/merge-recipe.md)**: never `git merge`, `git pull`, `git push` or `HEAD:<destino>` by hand. If the environment or the remote stops it, [recipe](references/merge-recipe.md#denied-by-the-environment). Into `main` and with a tag, a hotfix alone merges, with the dev-lead's words and the pre-check's answer, as the [recipe](references/merge-recipe.md#hotfix) says. Any other push and any PR are always confirmed. With `merge.removeWorktree: true`, the worktree goes as «Removing the worktree» of the [recipe](references/merge-recipe.md#removing-the-worktree) says.
8. **Kit ticket** — offer the kit's improvement ticket with `sdd-feedback` in this same session, unless this change already has its own, even if the session made another's: clearing the context loses what was learned about the kit. The offer goes in the final message and names each `Retro: … → ticket del kit` line. It isn't a gate: without an answer the close ends and leaves nothing pending.
9. **Final message** — the last message. What the close has to say gathers here, even if it was born earlier. In this order, only what has content:
   1. If you made the deferral trigger concrete: «disparador: <…>, a cargo de <…>; lo elegí yo porque tu frase no lo nombraba, corrígelo si no es el tuyo».
   2. The decisions taken without the dev-lead: name each one of `walkthrough.md` (`Patch only:` the `sin el dev-lead` lines of `Decisiones`, `patch.md` §3) in one line, even if another session wrote it; don't summarize. With `field`, a 🦆 before, and the rulings under «✋ Me salí del plan en…».
   3. Each instruction the dev-lead gave before the close and how it ended.
   4. What's pending, with the evidence of a denied or failed merge or push; and, if step 6 ran the gate, its `Gate:` line and its sha; and, if step 6 resolved a conflict, its `Conflicto de la base resuelto por el agente: …` line, as written.
   5. What's next, with `parent:` or `proposal:` in the artifact ([records.md](references/records.md#whats-next-in-the-family)), with these lines, literal: «Para acabar la `<madre>` falta: `<id>` — `<título de la fila>`[, …]. Recomiendo `<id>`, la primera del roadmap sin dependencias abiertas; su prompt lo da `sdd-roadmap`: «dame el prompt de la `<id>`».»; all blocked, instead of the recommendation, «Ninguna se puede arrancar todavía: `<id>` espera a `<ids>`.»; none open and the mother open, «La `<madre>` ya no tiene piezas abiertas: su fila se puede cerrar.» Don't mark the mother's row.
   6. The ticket offer, if step 8 makes it.
   7. The done line, always last. With the branch merged into `merge.into` and without uncommitted changes of the change itself; the kit ticket doesn't count: `**Terminado.** <rama> fusionada en <destino> (<hash>) · push: <hecho a <remoto>/<destino> | no hecho: <motivo>> · puedes borrar el worktree <ruta>`. Otherwise: `**No terminado.** Falta <qué>. No borres todavía el worktree <ruta>.` A failed or unauthorized push goes in «push: no hecho» and doesn't change the line; «No terminado» is only for a missing merge or uncommitted changes of the change itself. Only a linked worktree is offered for removal: if the branch is in the repo's main checkout (`git rev-parse --git-dir` and `--git-common-dir` give the same path), the line ends at the push.

   The kit ticket goes where the project's instructions send it (`CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md`); with none, commit it on the branch and merge it with `sdd merge` as step 7 says, so removing the worktree doesn't lose it: the message that writes it ends with the done line again, with the new merge's hash. If git ignores its folder, say where the ticket is and don't merge again.

## Red flags — STOP, you haven't closed

- You're about to write a record with uncommitted changes other than the drafts of step 0, or without `sdd merge --check`.
- `Feature only:` you're about to merge without `walkthrough.md`, or the spec is still `draft` and you haven't pointed it out.
- The real time is blank, or the estimation-log lacks this change's row (with the estimation module on).
- `Feature only:` you wrote the `Retro:` line without walking the session's signals.
- You haven't touched the roadmap.
- You wrote into `operations.md` or `architecture.md` a value that already lives in `capabilities/` (a time, a limit, a quota).
- A structural learning stays in the walkthrough, or in `operations.md`, because the project has no `architecture.md`.
- You wrote a workaround into `operations.md` without its debt row.
- You're about to merge without step 1's record, without the user deciding it or the `merge` block declaring it, or into `main`.
- You're about to merge, other than a hotfix, without reading the base's CI that §Testing declares, or over its `failure`.
- `Patch only:` `patch.md` without the fix's commit hash.

| Excuse | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| "The user asked me to close: that's validating" | Asking to close is an order, not a test: validating is saying what they tried and that it works. Step 1 says who validates in each mode; with `manual`, the change waits for it, and «lo pruebo mañana» with an owned trigger is a deferred validation (🧪). |
| "It's a lite feature, the close goes light too" | Lite cheapens planning, not verification. Smoke, walkthrough with real time and roadmap still apply. |
| "The user is in a rush: minimal walkthrough and done" | The whole checklist fits in minutes; what's skipped today drifts tomorrow. |
| "The `merge` block and `delegate` let me merge without asking" | They authorize the merge, not the validation: who validates is step 1's mode, and with `manual` in `delegate` the close waits for the user. |
| "The roadmap will get updated" | The row is 30 seconds now; later nobody remembers. |
| "The suite was green earlier in the session" | Green proves the tree it ran on and no other: the suite runs on the tree that merges. |
