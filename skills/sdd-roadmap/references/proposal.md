# The proposal of something big

- The proposal lives in `<ts>-proposal-<id>-<slug>/proposal.md` (under `.docs/sdd/changes/`), traced from `proposal-template.md` of the `sdd-templates` skill with `source: interview`.
- Every business rule carries an example with input and output data.
- The split lists the features in their order, each with its id and its dependency.
- Each feature is a roadmap row: the proposal's path goes in the `**Requisitos:**` of its brief and the dependency in «Tras».
