# Migration to v2.0.0 — ids mode, control keys, configuration and memory; capabilities without history and with a purpose, and feature skills

Applies to any project whose `.docs/sdd/sdd-kit.json` lacks the `ids` field, `control.profile`, a
complete `merge` block, `merge.push` with the complete `merge` block, the brake keys (`control.maxParallelAgents`, `control.silence.*`), or `execution`, or whose
`.claude/settings.json` doesn't disable the automatic memory or whose `.gitignore` doesn't ignore the tools'
temporary files or the personal preferences.

Applies also to any project with `.docs/sdd/capabilities/` whose capabilities have the `## Historial` section or don't have `## Propósito`. Since v2.0.0 a capability opens with its purpose and carries, besides, only its requirements and its rules: who changed what is told by git and by the «Capacidades» block of each spec or `patch.md`.

Applies also to any project whose live docs cite `sdd-start-task` or `sdd-end-task`: since v2.0.0 the kit's unit of work is called feature, and those skills are `sdd-start-feature` and `sdd-end-feature`. The plan's tasks keep their name.

Applies also to any project whose `.claude/settings.json` doesn't declare the `superpowers-marketplace` marketplace: since v2.0.0 the dependency on superpowers comes from there.

A project already initialized with kit v2.0.0 or later gets all this in its own init and will find nothing to do here: every step is skipped by its predicate.

1. **Numbering mode.** If `.docs/sdd/sdd-kit.json` has no `ids` field: **gate** — invoke the `sdd-config` skill, which presents the two modes with the question of its catalog, and write in `ids.mode` the dev-lead's answer. If the dev-lead isn't there, the step stays **explicitly pending**: no answer is made up, the project keeps working in `tracker` meanwhile (no `ids` field = `tracker`, Art. IV), and the report notes how to resume it: invoke `sdd-init-brownfield` again on this project.
2. **Control keys.** If any key is missing —`control.profile`, a complete `merge` block,
   `merge.push` (only with the complete `merge` block, whether it was already there or this step leaves it),
   `control.maxParallelAgents` and `control.silence.*`, or `execution`—, invoke the `sdd-config` skill: it asks,
   **one per turn** and with its recommendation and its reason, the questions of its catalog that match what
   is missing, the same as in the inits, and writes in `sdd-kit.json` only what the dev-lead answers. What
   was already there isn't asked. If the dev-lead isn't there,
   the step stays **explicitly pending**: the project keeps working with the defaults
   (`control.profile: "delegate"`, the brakes of the keys table and `execution: auto`), with step 10 of the feature close
   asking the merge policy, as today, and without push; the report notes how to resume it.
3. **Project configuration.** `.claude/settings.json`: if it doesn't have `"autoMemoryEnabled": false`, it's created or
   merged with that key without touching the others. If it has `"autoMemoryEnabled": true`, ask before
   changing it; if the dev-lead says no, leave it and the report notes it. `.gitignore`: add
   `.playwright-mcp/`, `.superpowers/` and `.docs/sdd/sdd-kit.local.json` if they're missing, without duplicating lines; create it if it doesn't exist. If everything was already there,
   skip the step and say so. No gate: it's the same the init leaves.
4. **Automatic memory.** The folder is `~/.claude/projects/<project>/memory/`, or the one of `autoMemoryDirectory`
   if the project redefines it; all the worktrees of the repo share it. Each entry is a memory file
   indexed in `MEMORY.md`. If the folder doesn't exist or has no entries, skip the step and say so. If it
   has them: **gate** — present a table with each entry and the anchor document it would go to, or «ya está en
   `<doc>`» if its content already lives in the docs, and wait for the «sí». After the «sí», dump the missing ones and delete
   only the entries dumped or already present, with their line of `MEMORY.md`; an entry that wasn't dumped isn't
   deleted. If the dev-lead isn't there, the table goes in the report anyway and nothing is deleted: the step stays
   **explicitly pending** and the report notes how to resume it (invoke `sdd-init-brownfield` again on this
   project).
5. **`sdd-start-release` retired.** No predicate and no changes to the project: the report says that «prepara la
   release», replanning and putting work in the roadmap move to `sdd-roadmap`, and that the minutes of a meeting with the
   client are gathered by `sdd-roadmap` in a proposal (`specs/<ts>-proposal-<id>-<slug>/proposal.md`). No gate.
6. **Capability history.** If any `.docs/sdd/capabilities/*.md` has a `## Historial` section: delete the whole section, from its title to the next `##` section or the end of the file, and nothing else. No gate: the history stays in git and the dev-lead sees it in the diff. If `capabilities/` doesn't exist, or no capability has a history, skip the step and say so.
7. **Capability purpose.** If any `.docs/sdd/capabilities/*.md` has neither the `## Propósito` section nor the mark `> **No es una capacidad.**`: write it after the title, as the first section, with one or two sentences of 300 characters at most that say what the capability covers. They come from the paragraph under the title, if there is one, without the provenance («La declaró la spec de la task…»), and the paragraph goes away; without a paragraph, they come from the titles of its requirements. No gate: the report lists the capabilities whose purpose was written. If `capabilities/` doesn't exist, or all of them have a purpose, skip the step and say so.
8. **Names of the feature skills.** If `CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md`, any `.docs/sdd/*.md` that isn't `changelog.md`, `client-changelog.md` or `roadmap.md`, or any `.docs/sdd/capabilities/*.md` cites `sdd-start-task` or `sdd-end-task`: replace them with `sdd-start-feature` and `sdd-end-feature`, and nothing else. No gate: it's a name, and the dev-lead sees it in the diff; the report lists the changed files. The history isn't touched: `specs/`, `changelog.md`, `client-changelog.md`, `roadmap.md`, `releases/` and `kit-feedback/` describe what was already done with the names of the time, and a `*-task-*` folder isn't renamed: the kit reads it as legacy. If no live doc cites those names, skip the step and say so.
9. **Superpowers marketplace.** Since v2.0.0 the kit resolves superpowers against `superpowers-marketplace` (obra/superpowers-marketplace), not against `claude-plugins-official`, which pins superpowers to a commit and lags behind the new versions. If `.claude/settings.json` doesn't have `extraKnownMarketplaces.superpowers-marketplace`: create it, or merge it without touching the other keys or entries, with the source `{"source": "github", "repo": "obra/superpowers-marketplace"}`. No gate: the dev-lead sees it in the diff. If `claude plugin marketplace list` doesn't show `superpowers-marketplace`, run `claude plugin marketplace add obra/superpowers-marketplace`: without it, the kit's dependency isn't installed on this machine. If the key is already there and the marketplace appears in the list, skip the step and say so.
10. **Marker.** Write `.docs/sdd/sdd-kit.json`: `{ "version": "2.0.0", "channel": "plugin" | "cli", "updated": "<hoy>", "ids": { "mode": "tracker" | "sequence" } }`, without touching the other keys,
   adding in `"control"` the fields step 2 obtained (`profile`, `maxParallelAgents`,
   `silence.betweenStepsMinutes`, `silence.longCommandMinutes`) and `"merge": { "into", "noFf", "removeWorktree" }`
   only if it obtained the whole policy. If the dev-lead answered «sí» or «no» to the push question, `"push"` goes
   inside `merge`: in the block this step writes, or added to the `merge` block that was already complete,
   without touching its other fields. If step 2 obtained `execution`, write it at the root as `"execution"`.

`specs/`, `changelog.md`, `roadmap.md` and `releases/` aren't touched: they describe what was already delivered and rewriting them would falsify the history.

**Writes**: `sdd-kit.json` → `version`, `channel`, `updated`, `ids.mode`, `control.profile`, `control.maxParallelAgents`, `control.silence.betweenStepsMinutes`, `control.silence.longCommandMinutes`, `merge`, `merge.push`, `execution`; `.claude/settings.json` → `autoMemoryEnabled`, `extraKnownMarketplaces.superpowers-marketplace`; `.gitignore` → `.playwright-mcp/`, `.superpowers/`, `.docs/sdd/sdd-kit.local.json`. Also deletes the history of each capability (step 6), writes the purpose of those that lack it (step 7) and changes to the feature names the skills the live docs cite (step 8).

## Verification

- `(Get-Content .docs/sdd/sdd-kit.json -Raw | ConvertFrom-Json).ids.mode` returns `tracker` or `sequence`.
- `(Get-Content .docs/sdd/sdd-kit.json -Raw | ConvertFrom-Json).control.profile` returns a valid profile
  (`pair`, `delegate` or `unattended`), or the report lists it as pending.
- `(Get-Content .docs/sdd/sdd-kit.json -Raw | ConvertFrom-Json).control.maxParallelAgents` returns an integer,
  or the dev-lead answered «no sé» (the defaults apply), or the report lists the brakes as pending.
- With the complete `merge` block, `(Get-Content .docs/sdd/sdd-kit.json -Raw | ConvertFrom-Json).merge.push`
  returns `True` or `False`, or the dev-lead answered «no sé», or the report lists the question as pending.
- The `execution` key: `(Get-Content .docs/sdd/sdd-kit.json -Raw | ConvertFrom-Json).execution` returns
  `auto`, `native` or `subagent`, or the dev-lead answered «no sé» (`auto` applies), or the report lists the question
  as pending.
- `(Get-Content .claude/settings.json -Raw | ConvertFrom-Json).autoMemoryEnabled` returns `False`, or the report
  notes that the dev-lead left it at `true`.
- `Select-String -Path .gitignore -Pattern '^\.playwright-mcp/$', '^\.superpowers/$', '^\.docs/sdd/sdd-kit\.local\.json$'` returns one line of each.
- The memory folder has no entries that step 4 dumped, or the report lists the step as pending.
- `sdd capability check --path .docs/sdd` writes `Capacidades válidas: <n>` or `Sin capacidades que validar`. If it fails for something other than the history or the purpose —a `**Reglas de la capacidad**` block pasted from a delta, a requirement without a scenario—, the report lists it as pending for the dev-lead, without touching it.
- `Select-String -Path .docs/sdd/capabilities/*.md -Pattern '^## Historial'` returns nothing.
- `git grep --untracked -nE 'sdd-(start|end)-task' -- CLAUDE.md AGENTS.md ':(glob).docs/sdd/*.md' ':(glob).docs/sdd/capabilities/*.md' ':!.docs/sdd/changelog.md' ':!.docs/sdd/client-changelog.md' ':!.docs/sdd/roadmap.md'` returns nothing, also without `AGENTS.md`.
- The `*-task-*` folders of `specs/` still exist.
- `(Get-Content .claude/settings.json -Raw | ConvertFrom-Json).extraKnownMarketplaces.'superpowers-marketplace'.source.repo` returns `obra/superpowers-marketplace`, and `claude plugin marketplace list` shows `superpowers-marketplace`.
- `.docs/sdd/sdd-kit.json` exists with `"version": "2.0.0"`.
- `git log --diff-filter=M --name-only -1 -- .docs/sdd/changelog.md .docs/sdd/roadmap.md` doesn't show this migration: neither of the two was touched.
