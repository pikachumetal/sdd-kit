- **Deviation**: a change to the approved spec (a requirement, a THEN, the Scope, a «No entra» or «Dónde se prueba»). A Scope item marked «si el RED lo exhibe» with a clean RED at n = 3 goes out with a `Ruling:`; anything else is a deviation. Leaving the plan without changing the spec (a file under «NO se tocan», another order, a fix of the thread) is a `Ruling:`, not a deviation. In `pair` and `delegate`, leave the correction in its place in `spec.md` and its «Enmiendas» line uncommitted; if it changes the delta, one `sonnet` reviewer (`sdd-kit:effort-medium`) with the seven points of `review-spec.md` of `sdd-propose` reads it first, its findings in the ✋. Stop with 🦆 and ✋ and wait; approved, what touches a closed task goes to a new task at the end with its commit (`Task 3 — enmienda <fecha>: <qué>` after Task 2), not reopened, and `tasks.md` marks the closed task `afectada por enmienda <fecha> → Task 3`. In `unattended`, apply the most conservative option the same way, marked `sin aprobar`, and go on. A REFACTOR or fix round whose guidance or behavior adds or changes a THEN or an AND, even one left unwritten («don't write until the user confirms» fixing a smoke that «only says warn»), is a deviation, not a ruling: stop before committing it (`tests/sdd-deviations-red.md`, k2). One case goes on: an AND or a THEN that only applies, literally, a line of the spec's «Decisiones tomadas con el dev-lead». Write it in its place in `spec.md`, its «Enmiendas» line marked `sin aprobar`, commit it with the round and name it in ✋; a `Ruling:` hides the amendment from the dev-lead (`tests/sdd-deviations-0222-red.md`, k3). A value, case or text that line doesn't say: stop.
